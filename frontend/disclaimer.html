General Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 2026
Our Portfolio and Media Kit
Important Employment Notice: CorporateGuild is an independent career intelligence and discovery platform. We do not charge candidates any fees for job applications or hiring recommendations. Never pay any fee or deposit for an interview or job offer.

1. Nature of Job Listings

All job postings, vacancy details, requirements, and links published on our Job Search Portal are aggregated from publicly available verified career feeds and official employer portals. Postings are refreshed every 10 minutes to maintain maximum accuracy.

While we endeavor to keep all information up-to-date and accurate, CorporateGuild makes no warranties of any kind regarding completeness, validity, employer response rates, or the ongoing availability of listed positions.

2. No Direct Hiring Affiliation

Unless explicitly marked as a "Sponsored Recruitment Partner", CorporateGuild does not act as an agent, recruiter, or official representative of the companies featured on the portal. All trademarks, company logos, and brand names are the intellectual property of their respective owners.

3. Application Links & Third-Party Sites

Clicking "Direct Apply" directs applicants to the respective employer's official careers website or ATS application endpoint. CorporateGuild has no control over the privacy practices, content, or verification processes of external sites.

4. Data Storage & User Experience Commitment

As noted in our Privacy Policy, all user-related data stored on CorporateGuild (search history, session preferences, and registration credentials) is retained strictly for internal analytics and to deliver a fast, responsive user experience. We do not share or monetize candidate information.

5. Inquiries & Takedown Requests

If you are an employer and wish to update, modify, or remove a job listing from our indexing queue, please reach out via our official Contact Desk, or explore partnership opportunities in our Creator Media Kit.
